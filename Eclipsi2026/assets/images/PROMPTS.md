## Perseides · `perseides-sant-jeroni.webp`

> Use case: scientific-educational. Asset type: mobile app section illustration for adult logistics. Primary request: safe family Perseid meteor observation at a quiet rural picnic clearing near an ermita in the Ribera d'Ebre, one adult and two children aged about 10 and 12 lying comfortably on mats and looking at a wide night sky, a normal family car parked on a paved turnout at the edge, one dim red lantern. Style/medium: polished editorial digital illustration with subtle paper grain, same visual family as a modern astronomy field guide. Composition/framing: wide cinematic landscape, family small in lower third, broad starry sky dominates, several realistic short Perseid meteor trails. Lighting/mood: deep navy and indigo night, restrained turquoise ground accents, warm coral-red lantern, calm and practical. Safety constraints: no campfire, no road, no telescopes, no bright white lights, no text, no logos, no watermark.

## Laboratori · `laboratori-eclipsi.webp`

> Use case: scientific-educational. Asset type: mobile app illustration for children aged 10 to 12. Primary request: a young field scientist's eclipse mission shown as a dynamic tabletop scene: a hand reading an outdoor thermometer in shade, crescent-shaped sunlight projections made with a metal colander onto white card, a simple clock, eclipse glasses safely resting in a hard case. Style/medium: polished flat editorial illustration with layered paper textures and bold clean shapes. Composition/framing: landscape close-up, clear separate objects, energetic diagonal arrangement suitable for a mobile card. Color palette: navy, solar yellow, turquoise, coral, warm off-white. Scientific constraints: no one looks through the colander, eclipse glasses are clearly recognizable, thermometer scale visible but no labels. Constraints: no text, no logos, no watermark.

## Infantil · `infantil-eclipsi.webp`

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: mobile app welcome illustration for five-year-old children. Primary request: a warm, simple astronomy scene showing the Sun, Moon, and Earth aligned during an eclipse above a happy five-year-old child wearing certified eclipse glasses next to a supervising adult. Style/medium: friendly cut-paper children's illustration, chunky rounded shapes, expressive but not babyish, high visual clarity. Composition/framing: vertical-friendly centered composition with four clearly readable figures and generous spacing. Color palette: deep navy background, golden yellow Sun and corona, soft gray Moon, blue-green Earth, small coral accents. Safety constraints: the child and adult both wear eclipse glasses and face the Sun safely. Constraints: no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark.
